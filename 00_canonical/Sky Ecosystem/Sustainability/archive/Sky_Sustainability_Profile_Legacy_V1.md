# Sustainability Profile: Sky Ecosystem (Legacy V1.0)

**Date:** January 2026
**Framework Version:** 1.0 (Deprecated)
**Status:** Archived

## 1. Executive Summary
Sky (MakerDAO) operates as a **Type B: Yield-Capturing** protocol to sustain its peg. It relies heavily on RWA yields to subsidize the Dai Savings Rate (DSR).

**Verdict:** Transitioning. The protocol has achieved profitability (flow) but remains burdened by legacy debt (stock).

## 2. Economic Engine (Viability)
*   **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** ~3.75% (Exaggerated Estimate).
    *   *Claim:* Asset Yield 5% vs Liability Cost 1.25%.
*   **Liquidation Dependency:** < 0.4%.
    *   *Finding:* Not predatory.
*   **Revenue Source:** >50% from Crypto-Backed Loans (2025 pivot).

## 3. Governance & Adaptability
*   **Concentration:** High. Top delegates control the majority.
*   **Adaptability:** High. Can adjust rates (DSR) faster than competitors.
*   **Crisis Response:** Proven during FTX collapse (0 bad debt).

## 4. Key Risks
*   **Bad Debt:** $281M remains from previous cycles.
*   **Insolvency:** The Vow contract is technically insolvent (-$34M).
*   **Regulatory Risk:** Heavy reliance on USDC (PSM) and Real World Assets.
